THE PROBLEM

Intelligence is abundant.
Trust is scarce.

Single-model answers can be fluent, confident, and wrong. Serious work needs a system that makes the reasoning inspectable before the output becomes a decision.

01

Challenge the first answer

Independent agents are assigned competing roles so the system has to confront alternatives instead of optimizing for agreement.

02

Expose the evidence

Claims stay connected to the assumptions, sources, and gaps underneath them, so reviewers can inspect why the system believes what it believes.

03

Preserve the decision trail

The output becomes a durable decision record with disagreement, uncertainty, counterfactuals, and the final human call.

THE SYSTEM

A decision should survive
the room it was made in.

Interlinkd turns a question into a traceable reasoning process rather than a disposable chat response.

01
FRAME

Define the decision

Set the question, constraints, evidence, and what a useful answer must resolve.

02
CHALLENGE

Force independent arguments

Agents build competing cases, attack assumptions, and identify what would invalidate their own position.

03
ARBITRATE

Separate confidence from persuasion

An arbiter scores evidence, consistency, and uncertainty instead of rewarding whichever answer sounds best.

04
RECORD

Keep the reasoning

The decision, dissent, evidence, uncertainty, and review triggers remain inspectable later.
